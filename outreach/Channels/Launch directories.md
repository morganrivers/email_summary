# Launch directories

Free or cheap listings. Each is a backlink and a small discovery channel; none is a growth strategy. The value is cumulative and mostly SEO.

Pages for the ones worth thinking about: [[BetaList]], [[Uneed]], [[MicroLaunch]], [[Fazier]], [[Peerlist Launchpad]], [[StartupBase]], [[Product Hunt]], [[Show HN]], [[SaaSHub and AlternativeTo]].

## Recommended order

Widely repeated 2026 advice, and it matches your situation: BetaList first while you are pre-launch and the waitlist is the product, then a soft launch on TinyLaunch or MicroLaunch to shake out copy, then Uneed and Fazier once the landing page converts, then Peerlist and DevHunt because both suit a technical product, and [[Product Hunt]] and [[Show HN]] last.

## Honest expectation

A directory listing brings tens of visitors, not thousands, and the visitors are other makers. Do the whole set in one focused afternoon and then stop thinking about them. The reason to do it at all is that the backlinks and the AI-directory listings are what surface you months later when someone searches for a private email assistant.
