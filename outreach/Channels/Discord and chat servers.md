# Discord and chat servers

**Status:** not started

Servers are presence channels, not announcement channels. Being a known name in three servers beats being a stranger in twenty.

## TEE and confidential computing

| Server | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Phala** | You run on their stack | Get the invite from phala.com or docs.phala.com. Ask about attestation for your own stack; that is a builder question, not a pitch. [[Phala Network]] |
| **Oasis Network** | DeCC, privacy-first smart contracts | [[DeCC Alliance]] |
| **Secret Network** | DeCC, co-hosts the monthly X Space | [[DeCC Alliance]] |
| **iExec**, **COTI**, **Integritee**, **Automata**, **Marlin** | The rest of the DeCC crowd | 20+ projects in the alliance; pick two, not ten |

Reality check: this ecosystem is crypto-native. They will understand your enclave immediately and will not buy an email assistant. Value here is technical answers and credibility, not customers.

## Your own server

Deferred, deliberately. An empty Discord reads worse than no Discord, and a server needs daily presence you do not have while building. Revisit when there are enough waitlist signups that they would talk to each other. Until then a mailing list does the same job with none of the maintenance.
