**Status:** not started

Smaller reach than press, much higher trust transfer. A privacy podcast host vouching for you does more than an article.

## Approach

Pitch a topic, not a product: how split custody works, or what a professional's duty of confidentiality actually requires of a software vendor. Both are episodes; neither is an advert. Bring the bio, including the satellite and defence work, which is what makes a host take a technical claim seriously. See [[People]].
