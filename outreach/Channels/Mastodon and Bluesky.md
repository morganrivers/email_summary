# Mastodon and Bluesky

**Status:** not started

Where the privacy and infosec conversation moved, and where the German privacy scene largely lives.

## Mastodon

- **infosec.exchange** — the security instance. Researchers, practitioners, and the people whose opinions the [[Privacy Guides Community]] repeats
- **chaos.social** — CCC-adjacent, heavily German, overlaps with [[Chaos Computer Club]] and [[CCC Berlin]]
- **mastodon.social** and **social.tchncs.de** — general and German tech
- **mobilize.berlin** — where CryptoParty Berlin posts events. See [[Berlin]]

Federation means one account reaches all of it. An account on infosec.exchange or chaos.social reads as belonging; an account on a random instance reads as marketing.

## Bluesky

Growing tech and journalism presence, lower privacy-community density than Mastodon. Cheap to mirror posts there.

## X

Still where [[Founders and solo operators]] and the crypto and TEE crowd talk, including the monthly DeCC Spaces. Unavoidable for that reason and for no other.

## Use

Post build notes and the honest ledger, not announcements. This is also the cheapest place to run the search half of the work: watch for people complaining about AI email tools and reply as a person.
