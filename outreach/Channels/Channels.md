# Channels

Places to post, submit, or publish. Sorted by how much they punish self-promotion, least to most.

## Will happily take a submission

- [[Launch directories]] — BetaList, Uneed, MicroLaunch, Fazier, Peerlist, StartupBase and the rest
- [[Product Hunt]] — hold until real launch
- [[SaaSHub and AlternativeTo]] — evergreen, SEO, positions you against incumbents

## Will take a post if it is good

- [[Indie Hackers]]
- [[Show HN]] — one shot, needs a working product
- [[Press and newsletters]]
- [[Podcasts and YouTube]]

## Will take a person, not a post

- [[Privacy Guides Community]] — the largest personal privacy forum. Contribute first, mention the product only when directly relevant
- [[Reddit]] — rules vary wildly by subreddit and several ban promotion outright
- [[Discord and chat servers]] — presence over announcements
- [[Mastodon and Bluesky]] — infosec.exchange and the German privacy fediverse

## Sequencing that matches the soft-launch doc

1. Waitlist directories first, because [[BetaList]] review can take weeks and the queue is the constraint.
2. Community presence in parallel, starting with listening.
3. [[Show HN]] and [[Product Hunt]] only when the product works end to end.
