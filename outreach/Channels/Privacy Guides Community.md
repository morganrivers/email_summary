# Privacy Guides Community

**Status:** not started
**URL:** discuss.privacyguides.net

The largest personal-privacy forum, and as of 2026 effectively the centre of that world: Techlore shut its own forum down and it was scheduled for deletion on 2026-06-01, so that audience consolidated here.

## Why it matters

Two things at once. It is the best free adversarial review of your claims you will find, and privacyguides.org's recommendations carry weight far beyond the forum. Being listed there is a durable asset; being caught overstating there is a durable liability.

## How to engage

- Introduce the project honestly in the General category. Lead with the ledger: proven today, legally promised today, provable soon
- Expect immediate questions on: what the provider can see, what happens if the co-signer is compromised, why the refresh token custody split is not theatre, and why anyone should trust a hosted service at all
- Do not ask for a listing. Their tool criteria are strict and applying too early gets a no that is hard to reverse
