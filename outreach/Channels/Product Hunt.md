# Product Hunt

**Status:** hold
**URL:** producthunt.com

Still the largest launch venue. Deliberately on hold.

## Why hold

It is a 24-hour spike for a product people can use today. Spending it on a waitlist wastes the one launch you get, and the comment section will ask for a demo you cannot give. The soft-launch document says the same thing and it is right.

## When to use it

At real launch, once sign-up, drafting and billing work end to end for a stranger. Pair it with [[Show HN]] on the same week but not the same day, so a bad reception in one does not colour the other.

## Preparation that is worth doing early

- A hunter is no longer required, but comments on launch day decide ranking, so an audience must exist before launch day. That audience is built in [[Indie Hackers]], [[Reddit]] and [[Discord and chat servers]] over months
- Assets: gallery images, a 30-second demo, a first comment that tells the honest story rather than listing features
