# SaaSHub and AlternativeTo

**Status:** not started
**URLs:** saashub.com · alternativeto.net

Comparison and alternatives directories. Different from the launch tier: these are evergreen and search-driven, so they keep working long after a launch day.

## Why they matter more than their traffic suggests

People arrive at these sites already looking for a replacement for something. Listing Letterlock as a privacy-first alternative to the mainstream AI email assistants puts you in front of intent rather than curiosity, and it positions you directly against the incumbents catalogued in [[Competitors and adjacent products]].

## What to list against

Superhuman (now Grammarly-owned), Shortwave, Gemini in Gmail, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Proton Scribe. Each of those has an existing page with existing users reading it.

## Note

Both sites let users add products. Listings age well; write the description as if it will be read in two years, and do not date it with claims you might have to walk back.
