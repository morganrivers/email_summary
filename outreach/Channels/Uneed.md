# Uneed

**Status:** not started
**URL:** uneed.best

Founder-friendly Product Hunt alternative. Confirmed live 2026-08-06: 85,000 members, 653,000 visits and 2 million page views reported for 2026.

## Shape

Daily launches with homepage visibility, a community upvote loop, a daily newsletter, and a high-authority backlink (they claim DR 75). Free tier is a waiting line; paid tiers pick your date.

## Notes

- Longer discoverability than Product Hunt, because listings persist in categories rather than expiring after 24 hours
- Good second or third stop, after the landing page converts. A launch here on a page that does not convert wastes the slot
- Categories are broad (Development, Design, Marketing, Business), so pick the one where a privacy claim reads as a feature
