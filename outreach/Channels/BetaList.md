# BetaList

**Status:** not started
**URL:** betalist.com

The canonical pre-launch waitlist directory, running since 2010. Confirmed live 2026-08-06.

## Why it is first

It is the only directory on the map whose audience is specifically people who like signing up for things that do not exist yet, which is exactly what you are offering. It is also the slowest: free-tier review can take weeks. Submit before you need it, so the queue runs while you build.

## Notes

- Free submission with a long queue; paid options jump it. Exact terms sit behind sign-in, so confirm before assuming. See [[Unverified and open questions]]
- Wants a short description, a screenshot or logo, and a working landing page with email capture. The capture must work before you submit, since this sends traffic once and does not come back
- Audience is makers and early adopters, not [[Lawyers]] or [[Therapists and counsellors]]
