# Show HN

**Status:** hold
**URL:** news.ycombinator.com/show

The highest-variance channel on the map. A front-page Show HN for a privacy-architecture product reaches exactly the people who understand it. A flat one is invisible and cannot be retried with the same story.

## Why hold, and what changes it

HN is unforgiving toward half-built things and merciless toward overstated security claims. Two preconditions:

1. The product works for a stranger without you present.
2. The TEE-attack answer is written and you believe it. See [[Privacy-conscious technical users]].

## What this audience already believes

Useful context from recent threads, collected in [[AI email tools that broke trust]]:

- "Superhuman AI Exfiltrates Emails" (Jan 2026, 114 points) — the top comments argued that you can have secure systems or current-generation LLMs, not both, and asked why an email summariser needs broad access at all.
- Repeated Gemini and Copilot threads where the complaint is not a bug but the arrangement itself.

So the sceptical position is already articulated on HN, and it is *your* position. That is an unusual advantage: the story is not "AI email, but private," it is "here is the architecture that answers the objection you have all been making."

## Format

Title: `Show HN: Letterlock – Gmail drafting where the model runs in an enclave`. First comment does the honest ledger: what is proven, what is promised, what is not yet true. Answer every comment, including hostile ones, in your own voice.
