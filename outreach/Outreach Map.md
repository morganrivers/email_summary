## The two branches

The map splits along the split you already have in the product.

**Technical privacy branch.** [[Privacy-conscious technical users]], [[Founders and solo operators]], the TEE ecosystem ([[Phala Network]], [[NEAR AI]], [[DeCC Alliance]], [[Confidential Computing Consortium]]). Fast to reach, will stress-test the claims, low willingness to pay, high willingness to tell you that you are wrong. Use for validation and credibility.

**Confidentiality-obliged professionals branch.** [[Lawyers]], [[Therapists and counsellors]], [[Doctors and clinicians]], [[Accountants and tax advisers]], [[Journalists and their sources]]. Slower to reach, not on Hacker News, but they have a written duty that a normal AI email tool violates. In Germany that duty is criminal law rather than policy: see [[German professional secrecy holders]]. Use for revenue.
