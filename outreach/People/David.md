## The ask

Not his opinion. **Names.** Other people to talk to about security, which turns one conversation into a branch of this map rather than a data point. The soft-launch document lists this as the first community action for a reason: referrals compound and channels do not.

Concrete version: two or three people who would tell you honestly whether the custody split is real security or theatre, and, separately, anyone he knows who works inside a profession with a confidentiality duty.

## Practical

- Get a Signal number rather than continuing over email, which is both more secure and more likely to get answered
- Come with the one-paragraph boundary statement written, so the conversation is about the design rather than about what you mean
