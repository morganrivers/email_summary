## The referral pattern

The single highest-yield move in this whole vault is not a channel. It is asking one person who trusts you for two names. It compounds and it costs nothing, and it is the only way into [[Lawyers]], [[Therapists and counsellors]] and [[Accountants and tax advisers]], none of whom read Hacker News.

Ask shape that works: *"I am building X. I do not want to sell you anything. Who do you know who complains about their inbox and cannot put client information into ChatGPT?"*

## Your own materials

Two things every page here eventually needs from you rather than from them.

**The bio.** Factual, not boastful. The satellite and defence work is the credibility signal for a security audience: it says you have worked where getting this wrong has consequences. One paragraph, reusable for [[Y Combinator]] co-founder matching, [[Peerlist Launchpad]], podcast pitches and the landing page.

**The one-paragraph boundary statement.** Exactly what is attested today and what is not. The soft-launch document calls this the source of truth for every claim on the page. It is also what you say out loud when someone at [[CCC Berlin]] asks.
