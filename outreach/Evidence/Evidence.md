## What the sleuthing actually found

Your question was whether lawyers and doctors complain about email in a way that would show up online. Short answer: yes for email volume, and yes for AI confidentiality, but they are two separate conversations happening in two separate places, and neither is happening on the channels you currently frequent.

**The volume complaint is well documented and old.** Legal-profession surveys have said for years that lawyers feel inundated; accountants have hard numbers (100–200 emails a day, more in busy season). This is a real, dated, quantified pain.

**The confidentiality complaint is newer, sharper, and better for you.** It is not phrased as a complaint. It is phrased as a rule: ABA Formal Opinion 512, HIPAA business associate agreements, §203 StGB. Professionals are being told by their own bodies that they must know what an AI tool does with their data and must not simply trust a terms-of-service promise. That is a demand statement written by someone other than you.

**The gap between them is the opportunity.** Every mainstream AI email assistant answers the volume complaint and fails the confidentiality rule. That is the sentence the whole product rests on, and unlike most such sentences it is backed by citations rather than by vibes.
