# Berlin privacy companies

**Status:** not started

Berlin and Germany have a real privacy-email industry. These are neither competitors nor customers exactly; they are the companies whose users are already paying for the belief that motivates your product.

## Local

**Posteo** — posteo.de, Berlin. Paid email since 2009. Anonymous registration and payment, no IP logging, first German provider to publish a transparency report (2014), early full support for forward secrecy. Ideologically strict, small, independent.

**mailbox.org** — operated by Heinlein Support GmbH, Berlin. Servers exclusively in German data centres, no data leaving the country, green power. Since September 2025 positioned as a "digitally sovereign workplace": mail, calendar, video, storage. This is the most interesting company on the page, because a sovereign-workplace suite has an obvious hole where a private AI assistant would go, and their customers are exactly [[German professional secrecy holders]].

**Tuta** (formerly Tutanota) — Hanover, not Berlin. Largest encrypted email service in the EU. Also runs a content operation that publishes comparisons of private email services.

## Why they are on the map

1. **Distribution.** Each has tens or hundreds of thousands of users who already pay for privacy and already use email as their main tool. An integration or a referral relationship reaches more of the right people than every launch directory combined.
2. **Constraint.** Their end-to-end encrypted mailboxes cannot be read by an AI assistant at all, which is precisely why they have not built one. Letterlock currently works on Gmail. That mismatch is worth understanding before approaching anyone: the question is whether your enclave model is the thing that lets a provider like this offer assistance without breaking their own promise.
3. **Language.** Their marketing is the register German privacy buyers respond to. Read it before writing German copy.
