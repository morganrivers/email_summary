# CCC Berlin

**Status:** not started
**URL:** berlin.ccc.de
**Where:** Marienstraße 11, Berlin-Mitte

The Berlin chapter of the [[Chaos Computer Club]].

## Two formats that matter

**Club Discordia** — the public weekly meetup, Thursdays from around 19:00. Anyone interested is invited. No registration, no pitch, no agenda. This is the single lowest-friction recurring in-person opportunity on the entire map, and it is fifteen minutes from most of Berlin.

**Datengarten** — the chapter's public talk and workshop format, sometimes part of Club Discordia. Topics are privacy, cryptography, security, data protection. A talk here on split custody, or on what a TDX quote does and does not prove, would be well within their normal range and would put the design in front of people who can find holes in it.

## What to expect

Direct criticism, no politeness buffer, and no interest in your business model. Someone will say you should not be trusting a hosted service at all, and they will be arguing in good faith. That is the value.

## Adjacent

- **CryptoParty Berlin** — announced via mobilize.berlin/@cryptoparty, contact cryptoparty@enteig.net. Teaching civilians to protect themselves; a good place to hear how non-technical people describe their fears, in German
- **chaos.social** on Mastodon, for the same crowd online. See [[Mastodon and Bluesky]]
