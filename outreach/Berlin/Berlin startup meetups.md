# Berlin startup meetups

**Status:** not started

The founder layer of the city. Useful for co-founder search, feedback on the pitch, and the occasional first user. Not useful for reaching [[Lawyers]] or [[Therapists and counsellors]], who are not at these events.

## Recurring

| Group | Where to find it | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| **Startup Bootstrappers and Indie Hackers Berlin** | meetup.com/startup-bootstrappers-and-indiehackers | Solo founders and small teams, show-and-tell nights, pitch workshops. The closest fit to what you are doing |
| **Pioneer Pints** (Silicon Allee) | siliconallee.com | Monthly, Wednesdays, casual, no pitching format |
| **Founders & Friends Berlin** | meetup / Eventbrite | Themed evenings, market expansion and validation topics |
| **Merantix AI Campus events** | merantix.com | AI cluster in Dahlem, more corporate |

## Listings to check rather than memorise

- foundercal.com/cities/berlin — aggregated startup and tech events, dozens per month
- techinberlin.com/events
- berlin.de/deeptech/en/events — the city's own deep-tech listing
- dev.events/EU/DE/Berlin
- meetup.com and Eventbrite, filtered to Berlin

## Realistic use

One recurring group attended consistently beats five attended once. The bootstrappers group is the one to pick: right size of company, right stage, and a show-and-tell format that gets you feedback on drafts rather than on the idea.
