# Berlin

The in-person branch. The highest-signal validation available to you, and the cheapest: a coffee costs an hour, a Reddit post costs a week of waiting to be ignored.

Berlin is also, unusually, the right city for this specific product. Three things overlap here that overlap nowhere else: the German privacy and hacker culture ([[CCC Berlin]]), the confidential-computing industry (OC3 is held here), and the professions bound by §203 StGB ([[German professional secrecy holders]]), who are within cycling distance.

## How to use the city

Two different conversations, and do not mix them.

With technical people: bring the design and ask them to break it. With a lawyer, therapist or tax adviser you can reach through your own network: never mention the architecture. Ask what they do about client email today, what they were told they may not put into a computer, and what happens if they get it wrong. Then shut up. That is the conversation the rest of this vault exists to imitate at a distance.
