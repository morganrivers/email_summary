# Accountants and tax advisers

**Status:** not started

Possibly the best-fitting audience in this vault, and the one nobody thinks of first.

## Why the fit is good

- **Volume is documented and extreme.** Accountants and bookkeepers handle 100–200 emails a day in normal periods, and busy season multiplies it. The pain is not vague.
- **Seasonality gives you a deadline.** Tax season is a recurring, dated crisis. A tool that helps arrives at a moment when they will pay to make it stop.
- **Email is already understood as a compliance channel** in their trade press, not just a productivity one. Half the argument is pre-made.
- **They handle exactly the data that must not leak**: financial records, identifiers, whole client relationships.
- In Germany, **Steuerberater are named in §203 StGB** alongside lawyers and doctors. See [[German professional secrecy holders]].

## Threat model, in their words

Less "surveillance," more "if this leaks I lose my licence and my clients." Practical, unromantic, receptive to a straight technical answer.

## Where they are

- [[Reddit]] — r/taxpros (practitioner-only, strict), r/accounting (unverified, see caveat)
- **Accounting Today** — accountingtoday.com, the trade paper, runs opinion pieces on exactly this topic. See [[Press and newsletters]]
- **Karbon** magazine, **Firm of the Future** (Intuit) — practice-management content hubs, both publish on email overload
- **CPA state societies** and their technology sections
- In Germany: **DATEV** ecosystem, Steuerberaterkammer, and the compliance-vendor blogs that already explain §203 duties to this profession

## Timing note

Do not launch into this audience during their busy season, when nobody has ten minutes. Approach in the shoulder months and be established before the crunch.
