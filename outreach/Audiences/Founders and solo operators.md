# Founders and solo operators

**Status:** not started

Consultants, agency owners, indie founders, anyone whose inbox is their business development. The easiest audience to reach and the one most likely to try something on a Tuesday afternoon.

## Threat model, in their words

Weak. They will not buy on privacy. They buy on hours saved and on the drafts sounding like them, which is what the voice profile is for. Privacy is the tiebreaker, not the pitch, and pitching privacy to them wastes the conversation.

## Where they are

- [[Indie Hackers]] — indiehackers.com, and WIP (wip.co) for the ship-in-public subset
- [[Show HN]] and [[Product Hunt]]
- [[Reddit]] — r/SideProject, r/SaaS, r/startups, r/Entrepreneur (unverified, see caveat)
- X, which remains where this crowd actually talks despite everything
- [[Berlin startup meetups]] — Startup Bootstrappers and Indie Hackers Berlin, Pioneer Pints, Silicon Allee
- Paid mastermind communities: StartupSauce, FounderLed, Founders Network. Worth money only once you have revenue to discuss

## Why they are on the map anyway

Three reasons that have nothing to do with revenue.

1. They are the fastest source of feedback on whether the drafts are any good, which is the part of the product no amount of security work fixes.
2. They are where the launch directories point, so you will be in front of them whether you plan for it or not.
3. Co-founder search runs through this population. See [[Y Combinator]].
