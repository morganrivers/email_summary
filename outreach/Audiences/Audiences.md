# Audiences

Who might pay, what they are afraid of, and where they gather. Each page states the threat model in that group's own words, because the threat model is what you are testing when you post.

## The one that stands out

[[German professional secrecy holders]] is the only group in the table where using a normal AI email assistant is not a policy breach but a criminal offence, and where the exemption conditions are written down and can be met by a specific technical arrangement. It is also the group physically closest to you. If any branch of this map deserves more effort than its row suggests, it is that one.

## What to listen for

The question that separates the branches, asked in every venue: *what are you actually afraid of happening to your data?*

- "A company training on my mail" → privacy branch. Buys on principle, churns on price.
- "My client finding out their file went through a US server" → professional branch. Buys on liability.
- "Someone reading my drafts before I send them" → neither; that is a competitor complaint and it means they already tried something.
