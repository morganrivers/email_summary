# Doctors and clinicians

**Status:** not started

Lower priority than [[Lawyers]] or [[Therapists and counsellors]], for structural reasons worth writing down rather than rediscovering.

## Why this is hard

- Most doctors do not control their own email. Hospital IT does, and hospital IT buys through procurement cycles measured in quarters.
- Clinical messaging has largely moved off email into EHR portals (Epic MyChart and similar). The email pain is real but it is not where the patient data flows.
- HIPAA and BAA apply exactly as in [[Therapists and counsellors]], with a larger compliance apparatus behind them.

## Where the reachable ones are

Private practice, small clinics, and independent specialists, who do control their own mail.

- **Sermo** and **Doximity** — verified physician networks, heavily gated, vendor-hostile
- [[Reddit]] — r/medicine, r/whitecoatinvestor (the latter is finance-minded doctors who run their own practices, which is the segment that can actually buy) (unverified, see caveat)
- Medical association technology committees, similar shape to bar association tech sections
- In Germany: the Ärztekammer route and [[German professional secrecy holders]], where §203 StGB names doctors explicitly

## Verdict

Keep on the map, do not spend the first hundred hours here. Revisit if the [[Therapists and counsellors]] branch works, since the compliance machinery is the same and the second sale is cheaper than the first.
