# German professional secrecy holders

**Status:** not started

Berufsgeheimnisträger under **§203 StGB**: lawyers, doctors, psychotherapists, tax advisers, and their staff. This is the strongest audience fit in the vault and it is on your doorstep.

## Why this is different from every other row in [[Audiences]]

Elsewhere, using a careless AI email tool breaks a professional rule. Here it is a criminal offence. §203 StGB punishes unauthorised disclosure of another person's secret by a professional secret holder. Data-protection consent does not cure it: a DPA alone is explicitly insufficient once client or patient data passes into a third party's hands.

## The exemption, which is the actual product requirement

German practice describes a three-part test for lawfully involving an outside service provider:

1. The involvement must be **necessary for proper professional practice**.
2. The provider must be **bound to secrecy in text form** and **informed of the criminal liability** for breach.
3. The professional must **select and supervise the provider carefully**.

Note what this means for Letterlock. Points 1 and 3 are arguments; point 2 is a document you must be willing to sign. The cryptography helps you satisfy point 3 far better than any competitor can, and helps not at all with point 2. **The deliverable for this audience is a §203-compliant Verpflichtungserklärung plus an attestation story, in that order.**

The German compliance commentary is also unusually blunt about AI specifically: confidential data must not leave the protected environment, and professionals may not transmit client data to AI providers. Every mainstream AI email tool fails that sentence. You are the only shape of product that can argue otherwise, and the argument is exactly your enclave boundary.

## Where they are

- **Kammern**: Rechtsanwaltskammer, Steuerberaterkammer, Ärztekammer, Psychotherapeutenkammer. Each runs technology and data-protection committees and publishes guidance. Slow, credible, high-trust
- **beA** (besonderes elektronisches Anwaltspostfach) discussions among German lawyers, where secure electronic communication is already a daily topic
- German data-protection consultancies and their blogs (activeMind, Datenschutz-Guru, keyed, and similar), which are the sources professionals already read on this question. A guest article is plausible
- **DATEV** ecosystem for Steuerberater
- **it-sa**, Nuremberg, 27–29 October 2026: Europe's largest IT security exhibition, German buyers, where this framing is native. See [[Upcoming Events]]
- [[Berlin]] for in-person
