**Status:** not started
**URLs:** phala.com · docs.phala.com

Privacy-preserving cloud built on TEEs, and the platform your enclave image targets. Discord, Reddit and Telegram communities; get the live invite from their own site rather than a third-party listing. See [[Unverified and open questions]].

## Two relationships, not one

**As a user.** You have questions their engineers can answer and nobody else can: attestation of your own stack, what dstack guarantees, how the AppAuth contract interacts with your co-signer's independent allowlist. Ask them as a builder in their Discord.

**As a reference.** Phala needs applications that a non-technical person pays for. Letterlock is one. That asymmetry is worth using: case study, ecosystem grant, or a joint post about attesting a real product rather than a demo.

## Caution

Their marketing language is looser than yours. Do not let a co-marketing post claim end-to-end attestation you do not have. The honesty rules in [[Outreach Map]] apply to things written about you as much as by you.
