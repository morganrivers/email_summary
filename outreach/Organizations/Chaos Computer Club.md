# Chaos Computer Club

**Status:** not started
**URL:** ccc.de

Founded 1981. The largest and most influential civil-society organisation on technology, security and privacy in the German-speaking world. 25 regional Erfakreise plus smaller Chaostreffs. See [[CCC Berlin]] for the local chapter.

## Why it is on this map

Not as a marketing channel. It is the single densest concentration of people who will tell you whether your security claims survive contact with someone who does this for a living. It is also, in Germany, where technical credibility is conferred.

## Events

- **40C3**, 27–30 December 2026, Hamburg Messe. 17,000+ attendees. Tickets are scarce and sell out; watch tickets.events.ccc.de. Aggregators list €50–575, unconfirmed
- **Chaos Communication Camp** — outdoors, every four years, not 2026
- Local chapters run weekly and monthly events all year. [[CCC Berlin]]

## Rules of engagement

Commercial pitching is not welcome and will be noticed. Talks about how something works, including what does not work, are the currency. The right posture is a person who built something and wants it examined.
