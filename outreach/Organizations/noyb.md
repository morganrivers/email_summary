# noyb

**Status:** not started
**URL:** noyb.eu

None Of Your Business. Max Schrems's non-profit, Vienna. Enforces EEA data-protection law against private companies, pan-European scope, EDRi member since 2018. See [[EDRi]].

## Why it is on the map

They are the reason "US cloud provider processes EU personal data" is a live legal risk rather than a theoretical one, and that risk is a direct argument for your architecture with [[German professional secrecy holders]] and European [[Lawyers]].

## Two uses

1. **Language.** Their published complaints and analyses are the clearest available statements of what actually goes wrong when personal data reaches a US-controlled service. That is your competitive argument against the incumbents in [[Competitors and adjacent products]], written by people whose job is to be precise about it.
2. **Attention.** They act on specific companies. A well-documented account of what a mainstream AI email assistant does with mail contents is the kind of thing their world notices, though picking that fight is a choice with consequences and is not necessary to sell anything.

## Caution

Do not present yourself as GDPR-compliant-therefore-safe. This crowd knows the difference between compliance and confidentiality, and conflating them is exactly the incumbent behaviour you are criticising.
