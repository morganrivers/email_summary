# Confidential Computing Consortium

**Status:** not started
**URL:** confidentialcomputing.io

Linux Foundation project. The standards and vendor body for the category: Intel, AMD, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Google, IBM, plus smaller members joining continually.

## Events

- **Confidential Computing Summit** — June, San Francisco. The 2026 edition ran 23–24 June at the San Francisco Mint, themed on confidential agents and secure AI workflows, which is precisely what Letterlock is. Watch for the 2027 call for speakers
- **OC3**, run separately by Edgeless Systems, is in **Berlin** in March and matters more to you for that reason. See [[Berlin]] and [[Upcoming Events]]

## Why to engage

The consortium's members sell enclaves to enterprises. A working consumer-facing application of confidential computing is a case study they want and you do not have to pay for. A talk or a case study here is credibility that transfers directly to [[German professional secrecy holders]], who will not otherwise take an unknown developer's word for it.

## Newsletter

They publish a newsletter tracking new members and ecosystem news, which doubles as a source list of companies working on the same problem. See [[Competitors and adjacent products]].
