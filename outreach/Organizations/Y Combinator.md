# Y Combinator

**Status:** not started
**URLs:** ycombinator.com/apply · startupschool.org/cofounder-matching

Two separate things worth using, and the second is available today with no application.

## Co-founder matching

Free, open, 50,000+ profiles, vetted by YC. Onur's suggestion, and the most immediately actionable item in this vault.

- A profile is a forcing function for the bio you need anyway: what you have built, the satellite and defence background, what you want in a partner. See [[People]]
- What you are looking for is worth stating plainly: this project's gap is not engineering. It is distribution into [[Lawyers]], [[Therapists and counsellors]] and [[Accountants and tax advisers]], and someone who already knows one of those professions is worth more than another backend engineer
- YC's own advice on profiles: specifics beat adjectives, and a profile that names the idea attracts people who care about the idea

## The batch

- Winter 2027 deadline was not published at time of writing. Third parties estimate autumn 2026, roughly mid-September to early November. Verify at ycombinator.com/apply. See [[Unverified and open questions]]
- Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and late ones are still read
- A prior rejection does not block reapplication and reapplying is common

## Honest assessment

YC's current appetite is AI-native products with fast usage growth. A privacy-architecture email tool with a waitlist is a harder sell than a demo of drafts a lawyer actually sent. The application is cheap and the co-founder platform is free; the batch is not the plan.
