# Sovereign Tech Agency

**Status:** not started
**URL:** sovereign.tech
**Where:** Berlin

German federal body funding maintenance of critical open-source infrastructure. Formerly the Sovereign Tech Fund. Berlin-based, which makes it a local relationship as well as a funder. See [[Berlin]].

## Programmes

- **Sovereign Tech Fellowship** — pays maintainers, community managers and technical writers directly. 2026 round closed 2026-04-06. Compensation followed the German public-sector scale, roughly €64,000–82,000 per year full-time equivalent
- **Sovereign Tech Standards** — launched 2026, funds maintainers working on open standards with IETF, W3C or ISO. 2026 round closed 2026-05-19. Ten places, €4,800–5,200 per month for standardisation work
- **Investment programme** — direct funding for critical open-source components

## Fit

Indirect. They fund infrastructure that others depend on, not products. Letterlock as a whole is not their shape. A cleanly separated, reusable component might be: the split-custody token library, or the quote-verification policy code, if either were published as a standalone thing others could adopt.

Worth knowing about regardless, because they are in Berlin, they employ people who think about exactly your threat model, and their events and network overlap with [[Chaos Computer Club]] and the German open-source policy world.
