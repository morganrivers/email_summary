# Prototype Fund

**Status:** not started
**URL:** prototypefund.de

German public funding for early-stage open-source software in the public interest. Runs in rounds, historically around €47,500 over six months for an individual developer.

## Fit

The best-matched grant on this map for where you actually are: individual developer, open source, privacy, early stage, German resident. Where [[NLnet NGI Zero]] and [[Sovereign Tech Agency]] fund infrastructure others depend on, the Prototype Fund funds prototypes.

## Constraints to check before counting on it

- The funded work must be open source and public-interest, which is easier to argue for the custody and attestation machinery than for a subscription email product
- Residency and eligibility conditions apply
- Rounds have deadlines; check the site for the current one

## Adjacent German options

- **Sprind** (Bundesagentur für Sprunginnovationen) — much larger, much harder
- **EXIST Gründerstipendium** — German founder stipend, requires a university affiliation
- **Berlin Startup Stipendium** — Berlin-specific, university-affiliated. See [[Berlin]]
