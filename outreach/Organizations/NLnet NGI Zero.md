# NLnet NGI Zero

**Status:** not started
**URL:** nlnet.nl

Dutch foundation distributing European Commission money to free and open-source projects that make the internet more trustworthy and privacy-friendly. Grants are typically small (tens of thousands of euros), non-dilutive, and come with technical support: security review, accessibility review, licensing help.

## Fit

Strong on paper. Letterlock is open source, privacy infrastructure, European, and independent. NLnet funds exactly this shape of thing, in batches: 67 projects awarded in June 2026, 57 in April, 44 in March.

## Timing problem

The **thirteenth and final call of the NGI Zero Commons Fund closed 2026-06-01**. NGI TALER and Fediversity ran to 2026-08-01, and neither matches Letterlock. So the main door is shut at the moment this vault was written.

What to do: check nlnet.nl for successor programmes, since NGI funding has been renewed under new names repeatedly. Their "alternative funding sources" page also lists [[Prototype Fund]] and [[Sovereign Tech Agency]], which is where to look next.

## Constraint worth noting before applying

These funders expect the funded work to be open source and reusable. That fits the parts of the stack you already publish; it does not fit a proprietary hosted service. The custody and attestation code is the fundable component, not the product.
