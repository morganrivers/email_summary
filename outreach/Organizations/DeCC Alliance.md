## Honest read

This is a crypto marketing coalition as much as a technical one, and its members mostly sell to each other. Two reasons it stays on the map:

1. [[Phala Network]] is a member and you run on Phala.
2. It is the densest concentration of people who can critique an attestation design, and that critique is worth having before [[Show HN]].

Do not expect customers. Do expect one useful conversation about RTMR3 and compose measurement.
