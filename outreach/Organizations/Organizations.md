# Organizations

Bodies, alliances, funders and ecosystems. Some are potential allies, some are potential funders, some are just where the relevant people are already organised.

## Ecosystem you already depend on

- [[Phala Network]] — enclave hosting, and the AppAuth contract your co-signer allowlist mirrors
- [[NEAR AI]] — confidential inference providers behind `nearai-glm` and `nearai-gpt-oss`
- [[Confidential Computing Consortium]] — the standards body for the category
- [[DeCC Alliance]] — the crypto-native confidential-computing bloc

Being a real user of this technology, shipping a product a non-technical person can use, is unusual in that ecosystem. Most of it is infrastructure selling to infrastructure. That is leverage: you are the demo they lack.
