# Freedom of the Press Foundation

**Status:** not started
**URL:** freedom.press

Builds and maintains SecureDrop, used by 60+ news organisations in 22 languages, and runs a digital-security training team for journalists, documentary filmmakers and their sources.

## Why it is on the map

They publish an annual journalist's digital security checklist and evaluate tools for a readership whose threat model is genuinely adversarial. An entry in their world is credibility that carries into every other audience on this map.

## The honest position to take with them

Their advice for source communication is Signal, SecureDrop, OnionShare. It should stay that way, and you should say so. Letterlock is for the ordinary bulk of a journalist's inbox, not for source contact. Volunteering that boundary is what would make them take the rest seriously.

## Practical

- Their digisec blog and guides are the best available writing on threat modelling for non-technical professionals; useful as a model for how to explain your own boundary to [[Lawyers]] and [[Therapists and counsellors]]
- Their trainers talk to newsrooms daily, which makes them a listening post for what real confidentiality failures look like
