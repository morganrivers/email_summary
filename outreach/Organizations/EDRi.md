# EDRi

**Status:** not started
**URL:** edri.org

European Digital Rights. Network of 50+ NGOs, experts and academics defending digital rights across Europe. Headquartered in Brussels; **founded in Berlin in 2002** by ten non-profits from seven countries.

## Why it is here

Not a customer and not a channel. It is a map of the European privacy civil-society landscape: the member list is the list of organisations whose staff are potential users, whose newsletters reach the privacy-attentive public, and whose endorsement means something in the EU.

Members include [[noyb]] and Digitalcourage. Their "member in the spotlight" series is a readable index of who does what.

## Practical use

- Read the member list for the German and Berlin-based organisations, then treat those as local contacts. See [[Berlin]]
- NGO staff handle sensitive correspondence on small budgets, which makes them a plausible early user group even though they are not a market
- EDRi's policy positions on AI and confidentiality are useful language for the EU-facing version of your pitch
