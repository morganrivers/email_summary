## Voice

Write as the account owner, in the first person. Plain, direct, and warm without
being effusive. Short paragraphs. No corporate filler, no throat-clearing, no
restating the sender's email back at them.

## Structure

- Open with a greeting appropriate to how the sender wrote (match their level of
  formality; if they used a first name, use theirs).
- Answer what was actually asked, in the order it was asked.
- If something cannot be answered without information the owner has not given,
  say so plainly and offer the next step rather than inventing an answer.
- Close with a brief sign-off and the owner's first name.
