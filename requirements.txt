# --- PII analyzer (optional) -------------------------------------------------
# Off by default. Presidio imports spaCy behind it and costs ~470 MB resident
# before a model is loaded; en_core_web_lg is ~1.1 GB more at first use, per
# process that masks. That does not fit a 2 GB confidential VM, and the box is
# priced by the GB.
#
# Without these, pseudonymizer runs its regex-only path: the owner's own name,
# email and phone, known contacts, API keys, JWTs, private key blocks, email
# addresses and phone numbers are still masked. What is lost is NER, meaning
# names of people not already in the account's contacts. Measured recall on the
# public corpus is 96% with the analyzer and 74% without.
#
# Uncomment all three lines to switch it on. pseudonymizer.analyzer_available()
# picks the presence up on the next start, and the Settings checkbox stops
# being greyed out. Nothing else has to change on the Hetzner box.
#presidio-analyzer==2.2.363
#presidio-anonymizer==2.2.363
